# Flashscore football navigation and pair metadata

`listFootballCompetitionPairs(country, competition)` reads the pair links in a public football competition document. Copy both input segments from an existing `/football/<country>/<competition>/` link, such as `england/premier-league`. Results retain literal document order, not hydrated table order. The collection can be partial and has no schedule or freshness guarantee. Its label is the publisher's link text, not a home/away contract.

Pass a row's `first` and `second` unchanged to `resolveFootballPairMetadata`. They are complete slug-and-ID segments in literal URL order, not home/away order. The pair route selects a fixture and can select another event, competition or date than the listing. Check returned identity and competition. Returned home/away identities come from that selected event's metadata. `competition` includes the round label; `country` labels the competition. `competitionPath` is relative to `https://www.flashscore.com`.

No login is needed. Scores, status, kickoff, dates, schedules, odds and betting are outside these reads. A page title saying LIVE does not establish live play. Missing entities, other sports, historical event selection and pagination are unqualified. These reads do not establish sports truth.
