Use `subject` and `course` as the two path segments of an existing public course link. For example, `/math/geometry-tx` uses `subject=math course=geometry-tx`. Only lowercase letters, digits and hyphens are accepted. No account is required for the checked public courses.

Paths in results are relative to `https://www.khanacademy.org`. IDs are Khan Academy content identifiers. Course descriptions are short catalog text. Units follow the frontend's course ordering and can include teacher resources or simulations, so a unit is not necessarily a lesson or a count of lessons. English and US context are fixed in the captured request. Other locales, unlisted courses and full subject catalogs are unverified.
