## Learned operation and meaning

`listOffers(slug)` was learned from both real captures with no warnings and minimum tier 1.
It keeps direct regular buybox rows, public provider plan names, displayed monetization or
trial labels, visible price strings when supplied, and quality labels. Transparent placeholder
prices and promotional/similar-film cards are excluded. Physical media, cinema, rental,
purchase, subscription, bundle and free-trial labels must retain their distinct meanings.
Trials do not establish unconditional free viewing, and monthly amounts are plan charges.
No normalized currency, checkout price, playback, account eligibility or dated availability
claim is made.

The initial offline recipe check failed because Memento has three genuine free-trial labels
instead of the ordinary monetization text element. Inspection of the actual label markup
identified `offer__label__free-trial`; the corrected recipe preserves that duration label and
its following charge. This was an offline extraction correction before learning, not a failed
site call. Broad `a.offer` would also include promotions for similar titles, as the earlier
worker demonstrated.

The parameter is restricted to an ASCII movie slug and cannot carry a path or query. No
unavailable-film or empty-result expectation was established. TV titles, country switching,
pagination and interactive controls remain outside the proved contract.
